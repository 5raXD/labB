================================================================================
SIGNAL / DATA-FLOW SUMMARY
================================================================================
- 5 debouncers clean the buttons into single-cycle level signals.
- 'selected' chooses which stopwatch is being controlled:
        selected = 0  -> LEFT  stopwatch (Ctl + Counter run)
        selected = 1  -> RIGHT stopwatch (Stash advances on trig)
- Ctl is an FSM driven only when the LEFT stopwatch is selected
  (trig_ctl/split_ctl are masked when selected = 1).
- Counter produces the running BCD time on 'time_reading'.
- The local snapshot register implements the "split" view: while
  split_active is high, the display shows the frozen 'snapshot' instead
  of the live 'time_reading'. It is cleared on reset/init_regs or when
  counting stops.
- Stash is a DEPTH=5 FIFO of stored samples: 'btnD' (sample) pushes the
  current 'time_reading' in; while the RIGHT stopwatch is selected,
  'btnU' (trig) advances to the next stored sample.
- Seg_7_Display gets a 16-bit word {time_out, stash_out}, i.e. the
  live/split time on the left two digits and the stash readout on the
  right two digits, and drives the 4-digit 7-segment display.
